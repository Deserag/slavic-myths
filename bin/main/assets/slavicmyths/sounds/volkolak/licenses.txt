Slavic Myths 1.4.3: original Volkolak sound edits, 28 mono Vorbis files, 23 events.

Natural dog growl: HarveyBird (Frederick Bird), account coldvet, "Dog Growl",
https://freesound.org/people/coldvet/sounds/404920/ -- CC0 1.0.
Public preview source: https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/404/404920_5965692-lq.ogg
Edits: energetic excerpt selection, resampling, slight pitch/speed adjustment,
layering, envelopes, normalization, mono Vorbis encoding. Main creature cues
use 72% dog recording + 24% rough growl + 4% original harmonic layer.

Rough growl: egomassive, "Growl", https://freesound.org/people/egomassive/sounds/536761/
-- CC0 1.0. Its author credits "Monster Growl" by Cylon8472, also CC0.
Public preview source: https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/536/536761_1415754-lq.ogg
Edited and layered at 24% in most animal cues; 25% in curse_revive.

Wolf howls: U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, U.S. federal work, public domain.
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Wolf_howls.ogg
https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/8/87/Wolf_howls.ogg
escape_howl uses 11.2--12.7 seconds; howl_short uses 14.2--15.7 seconds.
These cues use 72% wolf recording, 24% rough growl, 4% original harmonic.
Existing Grey Wolf sound files are preserved.

curse_revive: 45% dog, 25% rough growl, 25% original noise, 5% original harmonic.
Windups, slashes, landings, stance movement, bad_blood_apply, grave rustle/dig,
skull unearthing and curse clearing: original deterministic procedural foley.
No sound copied from another game or mod. No silent substitute cues.
Every encoded file is decoded again and checked for peak <= -1 dBFS.
Detailed per-file durations, hashes, offsets and sources:
docs/verification/volkolak_1.4.3/resource_gates.json in the source project.
