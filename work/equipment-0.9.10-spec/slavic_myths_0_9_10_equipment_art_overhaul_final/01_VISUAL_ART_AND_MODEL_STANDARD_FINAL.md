# 01 — VISUAL ART / MODEL STANDARD

# SILHOUETTES

Dagger:
- shortest;
- compact guard;
- unique blade profile;
- not a smaller sword.

Sword:
- blade ~60–72% icon length;
- distinct guard/pommel;
- readable weapon mass.

Spear:
- shaft dominates total length;
- small head relative to shaft;
- visibly long in first person;
- not a short shovel.

Axe:
- asymmetrical head;
- readable edge/socket/haft.

Bow:
- distinctive limbs/grip/string;
- not recolored vanilla.

# PIXEL LANGUAGE

Use:
- hard-edged clusters;
- 3–5 major value bands;
- restrained highlights.

Avoid:
- photoreal gradients;
- generic MMO glow;
- noisy single-pixel detail;
- browser/mobile-game item frames.

# MATERIAL LANGUAGE

Steel: cold/dark neutral gray.
Silver: cooler/brighter, not pure white.
Perunite: cold blue-violet steel accent, not neon.
Fire gear: blackened metal + dark ember seams.
Solovey: blackened steel/leather + deep red + dark feather.
Depth: dark steel + blue-green patina.
Knyaz: cold steel + silver trim + deep dark-red cloth.

# ARMOR MODEL QUALITY

Helmet:
- real face opening/nasal/cheek geometry;
- no cube shell.

Chest:
- layered torso;
- shoulders;
- belt/cloth/plate separation.

Legs/boots:
- visible segmentation and greaves.

# HELD MODELS

Check first/third-person alignment.
Unique large weapons must not clip badly or occupy absurd screen area.

# SHIELDS

If still broken:
- fix blocking;
- durability;
- item/first-person/third-person model;
- enchant compatibility where applicable.

# QA

Compare every reworked weapon beside diamond/netherite equivalents in:
- inventory;
- first person;
- third person;
- dropped-item view.
