# 05 — STATS / BALANCE / ENCHANT COMPAT

# TIER LADDER

Iron
< early mod gear
< Diamond
≈ specialized Silver in ordinary combat
< Perunite / high mod gear
< strong specialized boss gear / Netherite depending situation

Boss gear wins through mechanics, not universally larger numbers.

# SPEED TARGETS

Dagger: 1.9–2.2.
Sword: 1.5–1.7.
Heavy axe: 0.8–1.0.
Polearm: 1.2–1.4.

Avoid high damage + very high speed combination.

# ENCHANTABILITY

Silver: moderate/high.
Perunite: high.
Boss gear: normal-to-high.

Do not block vanilla enchants merely because item is unique.

# TAGS

Audit actual 1.21.1 item tags used by enchantments and place mod gear correctly.

Do not invent invalid Minecraft tag IDs.
