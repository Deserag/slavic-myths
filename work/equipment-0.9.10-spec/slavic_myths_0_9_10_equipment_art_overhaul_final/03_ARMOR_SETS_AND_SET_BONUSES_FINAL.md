# 03 — ARMOR SETS & SET BONUSES

Bonus only active with complete 4-piece set.

# A01 — ДОСПЕХ КНЯЖЕСКОЙ ДРУЖИНЫ

Role:
heavy frontline defense.

Base:
Diamond Armor + Knyaz Upgrade Core.

Visual:
- dark cold steel;
- lamellar plates;
- chain gaps;
- deep red undercloth;
- silver trim;
- helmet with nasal and cheek geometry.

Stats:
- total armor 20;
- total toughness ~16;
- durability ~10–20% above diamond.

## ПОСЛЕДНИЙ РУБЕЖ

Trigger:
cross from >30% HP to <=30% from hostile damage.

Effect:
Resistance I 5 sec.
+30% knockback resistance 5 sec.

Cooldown:
45 sec.

Must cross threshold again after cooldown; no repeat-loop while permanently low.

---

# A02 — ДОСПЕХ СОЛОВЬЯ

Role:
mobility/ranged.

Base:
Diamond + Solovey Core.

Visual:
- blackened light helm/hood;
- dark leather/plates;
- deep red cloth;
- restrained black feather accents;
- no wings.

Stats:
- total armor 19;
- toughness ~12;
- full-set movement +5%.

## РАЗБОЙНИЧИЙ СКАЧОК

Activation:
sprint + crouch+jump or other conflict-safe input.

Movement:
~4–5 blocks forward;
~1.5–2 blocks vertical gain.

Cooldown:
12 sec.

Restrictions:
no flight;
no chain spam;
no swimming/riding activation;
does not inherently negate fall damage.

---

# A03 — ДОСПЕХ ОГНЕННОГО ЗМЕЯ

Role:
offensive fire / anti-wood.

Base:
Diamond + Fire Serpent Core.

Visual:
- blackened plate;
- dark burgundy cloth;
- sparse ember seams;
- not fully red/orange.

Stats:
- armor 20;
- toughness ~12.

Passive:
received burn duration -30%.
Not full immunity.

## ОГНЕННЫЙ ШАГ

After 2 sec continuous sprint:
temporary ember trail behind player.

Lifetime:
~1.5 sec.

Enemy contact:
small fire hit max ~1 damage;
burn 2 sec.

Same target:
max once per second.

Wooden entities:
may burn 1 sec longer.

Never place actual fire blocks.
Must be bounded/no persistent entity spam.

---

# A04 — ДОСПЕХ ГЛУБИН

Role:
water combat/defensive spacing.

Base:
Diamond + Depth Core.

Visual:
- dark steel;
- blue-green patina;
- restrained aquatic crest;
- rope/cloth details;
- not a fish costume.

Stats:
- armor 18–19;
- toughness ~14.

Full-set passive:
+15% swim speed;
~20% underwater mining-efficiency support if appropriate.
Do not duplicate permanent Dolphin's Grace.

## ТЕЧЕНИЕ

Trigger:
single incoming hit >=6 damage.

Cooldown:
20 sec.

Effect:
radial knockback radius 3.
In water: radius 4 and stronger.
Damage 0.

---

# BALANCE RULE

Knyaz = defense.
Solovey = mobility.
Fire = offensive environmental pressure.
Depth = water/control.

No universal best set.

All accept appropriate vanilla armor enchants.
