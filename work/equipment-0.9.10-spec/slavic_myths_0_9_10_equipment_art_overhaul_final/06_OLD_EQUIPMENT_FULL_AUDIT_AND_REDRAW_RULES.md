# 06 — OLD EQUIPMENT AUDIT / REDRAW

Find:
- same-shape swords;
- dagger = shorter sword;
- short spear;
- box helmets;
- recolor-only tools;
- broken shields;
- weak generic accessories;
- unreadable low-res food/ingredients;
- legacy poor textures.

Classify every item:
KEEP / POLISH / FULL_REDRAW
and
KEEP_STATS / BUFF_STATS / REWORK_MECHANIC.

# WEAPONS

If two weapons differ only by color, redesign silhouette.
Spear length must be fixed.
Mace must read blunt.
Staff must read long with focus/head.

# ARMOR

Colored cube-shell armor = FULL_REDRAW.

# TOOLS

Material family can share language, not exact shape.

# ACCESSORIES

Rings: thick readable ring.
Amulets: dominant pendant.
Belts: clear buckle/strap.

# FOOD

Only fix clearly weak/off-style legacy art here.
Do not turn this into full food patch.

# SPAWN EGGS

Normalize if visually weak.
Avoid overproduced custom mini-paintings.
