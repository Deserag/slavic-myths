# 08 — CODEX NO-FANTASY RULES

1. Do not invent extra equipment families.
2. Do not rename established IDs just for cleaner code.
3. Do not replace abilities with simpler substitutes.
4. Do not remove caps/cooldowns.
5. Bleed never affects `bleed_immune`.
6. Fire Strike never ignites terrain.
7. Thunder Axe does not need real lightning entities.
8. Solovey Bow never counts misses.
9. Solovey armor leap is not flight.
10. Depth armor is not permanent Dolphin's Grace.
11. Fire armor is not full fire immunity.
12. No set may dominate every metric.
13. Do not add many boss materials.
14. Audit existing drops first.
15. Do not implement rune/class/custom enchant systems here.
16. Prepare tags/data only.
17. Do not auto-run Minecraft.
18. Do not claim visual rework without actual textures/models.
19. If architecture blocks exact implementation, document blocker instead of improvising.
20. Final report must contain real IDs/numbers.
