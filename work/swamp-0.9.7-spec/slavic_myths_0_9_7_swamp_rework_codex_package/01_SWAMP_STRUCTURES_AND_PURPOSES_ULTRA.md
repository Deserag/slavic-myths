# 01 — SWAMP STRUCTURES AND PURPOSES — ULTRA DETAIL

Approved direction is based on the bundled structure concept sheets.
Do not simplify swamp structures into random huts.
Every structure must have a purpose.

## Overall generation philosophy
Use several layers:
1. micro POI (very small environmental stories);
2. small structures;
3. medium structures / structure clusters;
4. rare standout swamp locations.

Do NOT make all structures the same size or rarity.

---

# S01 — FISHER HUT / РЫБАЦКАЯ ИЗБУШКА

## Role
A practical swamp dwelling and useful early-to-mid exploration point.

## Visual identity
- wood hut on wet supports or a partially raised foundation;
- small boardwalk / dock;
- hanging lantern;
- fishing clutter;
- damp aged wood;
- one or two functional side props.

## Size
Approx footprint: 7×9 to 11×13, including porch/dock.

## Materials
Primary:
- dark wood family used by the mod/available target palette;
- stripped/log variants where useful;
- planks + slabs + stairs;
- trapdoors/fences/walls for trim;
- mossy stone or small stone footings only in limited support areas.

Water integration:
- support posts in water;
- reeds/cattails nearby;
- boat or net detail if supported by assets.

## Function
- ordinary useful loot;
- fishing-related items/materials;
- low risk;
- local atmosphere.

## Loot theme
- fish/fishing utility;
- rope/net-like materials if present in mod;
- herbs;
- modest supplies;
- map/note/clue chance if such system exists or is appropriate.

# S02 — WATCHTOWER / БОЛОТНАЯ СТОРОЖЕВАЯ ВЫШКА

## Role
Vertical observation point, clearer silhouette in swamp, useful landmark.

## Visual identity
- narrow timber watchtower;
- raised platform;
- ladder/stair climb;
- torch/lantern basket;
- guard clutter;
- sometimes linked to a small dock or platform.

## Size
Base footprint: 5×5 to 7×7.
Height: 9–16 blocks depending variant.

## Function
- landmark;
- vantage point;
- medium loot;
- possible hostile or semi-hostile encounter.

## Loot theme
- scouting supplies;
- arrows / hunting / survival supplies;
- coin and stored rations;
- one better chest in upper platform variant.

---

# S03 — RITUAL SHRINE / БОЛОТНОЕ СВЯТИЛИЩЕ

## Role
Mystical POI and stronger thematic anchor.

## Visual identity
- carved posts / stakes / low stone or wood circle;
- offerings;
- candles/lanterns;
- partially sunken sacred platform;
- eerie but not overbuilt.

## Size
Small-to-medium: 7×7 to 13×13.
Often open-air.

## Function
- thematic loot;
- ritual ingredients;
- encounter trigger possibility;
- lore atmosphere.

## Loot theme
- charms/relic materials;
- herbs;
- ritual items;
- rare swamp-specific special item chance.

---

# S04 — ABANDONED HUT / ЗАБРОШЕННАЯ ИЗБУШКА

## Role
Atmospheric ruin and minor exploration point.

## Visual identity
- damaged wood structure;
- partial roof collapse;
- overgrown edges;
- broken flooring over water;
- hints of old habitation.

## Function
- minor loot;
- story/environment;
- small hostile presence possible;
- possible clue to nearby shrine / stronger site.

## Loot theme
- low-to-mid salvage;
- small valuables;
- swamp herbs;
- one hidden cache chance.

# S05 — SUNKEN BOARDWALK / ПОЛУЗАТОПЛЕННЫЙ НАСТИЛ

## Role
Small path-like POI or connector setpiece.

## Visual identity
- broken posts;
- decayed planks;
- half-submerged walkway;
- reeds and lily pads integrated around it.

## Function
- micro-POI;

# S06 — SWAMP BOAT LANDING / ЛОДОЧНАЯ СТОЯНКА

## Role
Useful small utility POI on water edge.

## Visual identity
- docking posts;
- small covered shed or gear rack;
- one moored or wrecked boat impression;
- fishing/transport identity.

## Function
- minor loot;
- useful supplies;
- visual storytelling.

---

# S07 — RARE STANDOUT LOCATION: GREAT SWAMP SHRINE CLUSTER

## Role
A rare, higher-value swamp location to justify deeper exploration.

## Visual identity
- shrine core + 1–3 satellites (platform, altar, hut, watch post, path fragments);
- more complex silhouette;
- stronger loot;
- stronger mob presence.

## Function
- rare exploration reward;
- best swamp-themed loot;
- miniboss/elite encounter candidate if current mob set supports it.

Do NOT make this a giant fortress.
Keep it swamp-appropriate and fragmented.

---

# Structure usefulness rules
Each structure must provide at least one clear player answer to “why should I go there?”
Examples:
- useful chest;
- rare herb/relic;
- better encounter;
- clue/note/map;
- route landmark;
- material source;
- unique ambient scene.

No pure empty decoration.

---

# Structure rarity layers
Micro POI: common.
Fish huts / boardwalks / small landings: common-uncommon.
Watchtowers / abandoned huts / shrines: uncommon.
Rare shrine cluster: rare.

Do not oversaturate the swamp.
