# 03 — SWAMP TREES / NATURE / FEATURES — ULTRA DETAIL

Approved reference:
`references/ref_03_swamp_trees_sheet.png`

This file defines the swamp vegetation language.
The swamp remains vanilla, but becomes richer and more atmospheric.

---

# T01 — OLD SWAMP WILLOW / СТАРАЯ БОЛОТНАЯ ИВА

## Role
Main atmospheric tree type.

## Silhouette
- bent or leaning trunk;
- low hanging leaf curtains;
- visible roots meeting water or wet ground;
- asymmetrical crown;
- not too tall;
- broad readable mass.

## Target dimensions
Height: ~7–12 logs depending variant.
Canopy spread: ~7–13 blocks.

## Generation notes
- place often near water edge or shallow swamp water;
- roots may step into water;
- hanging vegetation should not become a full solid curtain everywhere;
- generate multiple shape variants.

---

# T02 — CROOKED SWAMP OAK / КРИВОЙ БОЛОТНЫЙ ДУБ

## Role
Accent tree with more dramatic form.

## Silhouette
- thicker trunk;
- strong lean/twist;
- broken or bent major branches;
- less even crown;
- stronger “old, struggling in swamp” feeling.

## Target dimensions
Height: ~8–14 logs.
Canopy spread: ~8–14 blocks.

## Use
- less frequent than willow;
- good near clearings, structures, or standout views.

---

# T03 — TALL BOG PINE / ВЫСОКАЯ ТОПЬ-СОСНА

## Role
Vertical accent and silhouette diversity.

## Silhouette
- tall thin trunk;
- sparse coniferous crown;
- hanging moss possible;
- roots on drier hummocks/islands;
- less bushy than willow/oak.

## Target dimensions
Height: ~12–20 logs.
Crown spread: narrow to moderate.

## Use
- occasional;
- especially in slightly drier swamp pockets or transition patches.

---

# T04 — DEAD DRY TREE / МЁРТВОЕ СУХОЕ ДЕРЕВО

## Role
Atmosphere and haunted mood.

## Silhouette
- bare branches;
- twisted form;
- minimal or no leaves;
- occasional moss remnants;
- good near ruins and eerie zones.

## Use
- uncommon;
- not too dense;
- works near ritual or ruined zones.

---

# NATURE MICRO-FEATURES

Add/support swamp detail such as:
- reeds/cattails;
- lily pads in better composition;
- roots entering water;
- moss patches;
- fallen logs;
- damp stumps;
- mushrooms in selected shady pockets;
- small stones / wet stones;
- shallow board pieces or environmental debris near structures.

These should enrich the swamp, not clutter every block.

---

# WATER EDGE RULES

Water edges should feel natural:
- roots partially in water;
- reeds concentrated in believable bands;
- occasional exposed mud/damp blocks if available;
- fewer abrupt empty shorelines.

---

# REPEAT REDUCTION

Tree variants must reduce repetition:
- multiple trunk bends;
- multiple crown densities;
- different branch break patterns;
- different hanging plant lengths.

Do not generate one template tree everywhere.
