# 02 — SWAMP MOBS / ROLES / REBALANCE — ULTRA DETAIL

User complaint: swamp mobs are currently too weak.
We may increase HP and improve design/behavior if justified.

This version should improve swamp encounters without turning all swamp mobs into raid bosses.

## General combat goals
- mobs must have clearer roles;
- mobs need more presence and durability;
- encounters need identity;
- spawn context must make sense;
- rare stronger encounters should exist.

## Baseline principle
Common swamp mobs should feel stronger than generic filler,
but not overshadow dedicated bosses from other versions.

---

# M01 — RUSALKA

## Encounter role
Ambush/control swamp spirit.

## Visual direction
- ghostly but still Minecraft-readable;
- long wet hair silhouette;
- pale or bluish desaturated body palette;
- swamp water integration;
- readable face/silhouette at distance;
- not photoreal.

## Suggested stat direction
If currently weak, raise HP into a clearly meaningful range.
Suggested target band:
- common Rusalka: ~32–44 HP.

Damage should be moderate, but danger comes from positioning/control.

## Behavior
Possible mix:
- appears near water, reeds, lilies, or shrine-adjacent areas;
- approaches through water better than on dry land;
- brief slow/drag/knockback/wet curse style mechanic if currently feasible;
- should not just walk like a zombie and hit.

## Spawn logic
- vanilla swamp;
- especially around water and ritual/swamp-atmosphere spots;
- more active at dusk/night if appropriate.

---

# M02 — BOLOTNIK

## Encounter role
Tanky bog lurker / area denial melee threat.

## Visual direction
- stockier silhouette;
- moss, mud, root, and wet wood/plant details;
- dark green-brown palette;
- heavy swamp mass;
- instantly readable as a bog creature.

## Suggested stat direction
Suggested HP target band:
- common Bolotnik: ~40–56 HP.

## Behavior
- slower but more oppressive;
- strong melee hit;
- may briefly root/slow player or throw mud/debuff if feasible;
- should be scarier in tight or wet terrain.

## Spawn logic
- denser swamp parts;
- near ruined shrines / abandoned structures / deeper marsh pockets.

---

# M03 — VODYANOY (if used as swamp/water elite or strong encounter)

## Role
Rare stronger encounter tied to water-rich swamp sectors.

## Suggested HP target band
- elite/common-rare Vodyanoy encounter: ~60–90 HP depending rarity and role.

If currently implemented as a more important creature, keep it below true boss scale unless explicitly intended.

## Behavior
- better in/around water;
- ranged or pull/push mechanic if feasible;
- should punish careless water fighting.

---

# M04 — SWAMP ELITE / RARE ENCOUNTER POLICY

If the swamp rare shrine cluster or stronger swamp sites need a more memorable fight, allow:
- elite variant of existing swamp mob;
- buffed HP;
- clearer loot reward;
- tighter spawn context.

HP should remain below major boss versions from other arcs.

Suggested elite band:
- ~80–140 HP depending encounter type.

Do NOT randomly place these everywhere.

---

# AI QUALITY RULES

Swamp mobs must not feel like generic passive wandering mobs.
Improve:
- acquisition range;
- pathing in/around shallow water;
- attack commitment;
- role clarity.

If dash/leap moves exist, ensure they face the target properly.
Avoid sideways/backwards execution bugs.

---

# REWARD LINK

Mobs should connect to structure/reward loop.
Examples:
- shrine mob protects ritual loot;
- hut area mob protects cache;
- elite water creature guards stronger reward.

No purposeless HP sponge.

---

# VISUAL POLICY

If mob models/textures are touched:
- keep them Minecraft-readable;
- preserve or improve silhouette;
- do not overcomplicate small details into unreadable noise;
- keep palette coherent with swamp environment.
