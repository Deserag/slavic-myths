# 06 — WORLDGEN / WATER / SPAWN INTEGRATION — ULTRA DETAIL

The swamp update must integrate naturally into marsh terrain.

## Water placement rules
Structures may be:
- partly above shallow water;
- on stilts;
- on muddy/drier islets;
- partly sunken if ruin type justifies it.

They must NOT:
- float inexplicably;
- carve absurd rectangular holes into swamp;
- create ugly unsupported corners;
- ignore water depth entirely.

## Terrain fitting
Use/support terrain adaptation:
- support posts can extend to bottom;
- stairs/boardwalks can bridge shallow uneven water;
- small platforms can adapt to slightly different heights;
- surrounding clutter can hide seams.

## Spawn spacing
Swamp should feel richer, but not crowded.
Define sensible frequency bands:
- micro POI: relatively common;
- small structures: common-uncommon;
- medium structures: uncommon;
- rare shrine cluster: rare.

## Mob placement
Common swamp mobs:
- distributed by sub-context.
Examples:
- Rusalka near water and lilies;
- Bolotnik in denser muck and around ruined sites;
- stronger water creature near larger water pockets or stronger shrine sites.

## Performance / sanity
Worldgen additions must remain bounded.
Do not create overly many structure placement attempts.
