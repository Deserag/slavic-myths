---

# 1. SURFACE MOUND

Do not replace the Kurgan with a giant fantasy temple.

It must still read as a burial mound.

Shape:
- low rounded hill;
- vegetation on top;
- local stones/markers;
- visible entry cut into one side.

0.9.5 terrain adaptation handles fitting the mound into terrain.
0.9.6 improves the authored entrance module.

---

# 2. APPROACH ZONE

Within approximately 8–16 blocks before doorway:
create subtle evidence of ancient site.

Possible:
- 2–5 scattered burial stones;
- one broken low wall segment;
- 1–2 standing stones;
- worn grass/path patches;
- exposed Cold Burial Stone fragments.

Do not clear a perfect plaza.

---

# 3. ENTRY FACADE

## Small Kurgan
Width:
5–7 blocks.
Height:
4–6.

Design:
two side piers;
stone lintel or shallow arch;
one carved block;
partial earth encroachment.

## Warrior
Width:
7–9.
Height:
5–7.

Design:
stronger piers;
3–5 block-wide opening;
stairs/slabs create depth;
two carved accents;
possible damaged marker stones.

## Great
Width:
9–13.
Height:
7–9.

Design:
deep portal recess;
layered arch;
carved blocks;
retaining walls;
short 3–5 block approach stair/terrace.

Still not a cathedral.

---

# 4. EARTH INTEGRATION

Facade must look embedded in mound.

Use:
- dirt/grass over upper stones;
- 1–3 block irregular side exposure;
- mossy/cracked blocks near earth contact.

Do not make a clean rectangular stone box pasted onto grass.

---

# 5. DOOR OPENING

Prefer no modern wooden door unless current design explicitly uses one.

Possible:
open dark threshold;
stone doorway;
broken gate remnants if assets exist.

Player should see darkness/vestibule, not the entire staircase to bottom.

---

# 6. VESTIBULE TRANSITION

Immediately after doorway:
3–8 blocks of horizontal/low-slope vestibule.

Then:
first junction or short turn.

Never:
door → 60-block straight descending stair.

---

# 7. LIGHT

Surface:
normal daylight.

Threshold:
one warm light source optional.

Vestibule:
1–2 dim sources.

Deeper:
lighting becomes sparse and room-dependent.

---

# 8. SOUND / ATMOSPHERE

Do not add a whole sound system if not already prepared.
But preserve space for:
- ambient cave sound;
- occasional water;
- fire/lantern;
- mob sounds.

No constant loud effect.

---

# 9. ENTRANCE VARIANTS

At least three authored entrance module variants if feasible:

A. Intact ancient entrance.
B. Partially collapsed.
C. Half-buried weathered.

Great Kurgan can have a fourth formal variant.

All connect to same connector contract so generator can choose.

---

# 10. BLOCK PALETTE

Small:
Cold Burial Stone 25–40%
Burial Bricks 20–30%
vanilla stone/cobble/stone brick 30–45%
carved <10%.

Warrior:
Burial Bricks more dominant.
Carved 5–12%.

Great:
more formal brick/carved framing, but earth/vanilla mix still present.
