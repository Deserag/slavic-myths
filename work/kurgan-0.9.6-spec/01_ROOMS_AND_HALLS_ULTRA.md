# 01 — KURGAN ROOMS AND HALLS — ULTRA DETAIL

Every room below is a distinct generation archetype.

Dimensions are interior clear dimensions unless specified.
Do not count wall thickness in interior dimensions.

General ceiling height:
- small utility room: 4–5 blocks clear;
- standard chamber: 5–6;
- ceremonial major hall: 7–9;
- Great Kurgan monumental variant: up to 10 only where justified.

No giant empty boxes.

---

# R01 — ENTRANCE VESTIBULE / ПРЕДДВЕРИЕ

## Purpose
Transition from surface burial mound to underground complex.

## Typical interior size
Small: 5×7 to 7×9.
Warrior: 7×9 to 9×11.
Great: 9×11 to 11×13.

## Shape
Rectangular with the long axis pointing inward.
Never use a perfect featureless cube.

## Floor
Base:
- 45% Cold Burial Stone tile/full stone;
- 30% vanilla stone brick;
- 15% cracked stone brick;
- 10% random worn slabs/gravel patches.

Create a centered 3-block-wide worn path toward inner doorway.
Floor edges may rise by slab steps.

## Walls
Lower 2 blocks:
- Burial Stone Bricks dominant.
Upper section:
- mixed Burial Bricks + vanilla stone bricks.
Corners:
- full Cold Burial Stone vertical supports.

## Ceiling
Low compared with later halls.
Use:
- stone brick ribs every 3–4 blocks;
- dark full blocks between ribs;
- occasional cracked patch.

## Entrance framing
Exterior side:
- two 2×2 or 2×3 masonry piers;
- shallow arch or lintel;
- carved block centered above opening.

Interior side:
- narrower arch leading to first corridor.

## Props
- one or two broken stone fragments;
- small urn niche in one variant;
- one unlit/low-light side niche;
- never place treasure chest directly in the first 3 blocks.

## Lighting
Sparse:
one or two lantern/fire sources maximum.
Entrance light should fade as player moves inside.

## Connections
1 exterior + 1–2 interior connectors.
Great Kurgan may have an immediate left/right side alcove but not a full branch maze yet.

## Used blocks
Custom:
- Cold Burial Stone;
- Burial Stone Bricks;
- Cracked Burial Bricks;
- Carved Burial Stone;
- corresponding slabs/stairs/walls.

Vanilla:
- stone;
- stone bricks;
- cracked stone bricks;
- cobblestone;
- gravel.

# R02 — CROSSROADS HALL / ЗАЛ РАЗВИЛКИ

## Purpose
A navigation hub that visually communicates multiple possible routes.

## Size
7×7, 9×9, or 11×11.
Great variant may be 11×13.

## Core design
Central floor marker/pillar/plinth.
3 or 4 exits.
Player must be able to see at least two directions immediately.

## Floor
Radial or cross-shaped pattern:
- central 3×3 Carved/Tile area;
- arms made of Burial Bricks/Stone;
- darker outer band.

Do not use bright symbols that look like modern signage.

## Walls
Each exit gets a slightly different treatment:
- intact arch;
- cracked arch;
- damp arch;
- narrower arch.
This helps orientation.

## Center
One of:
- 1-block carved plinth;
- broken pillar base;
- low memorial stone;
- no tall object blocking sight.

## Loot
Usually none.
Small chance for side urn alcove.

## Function
This room exists for navigation, not treasure.

## Used blocks
Custom:
Cold Burial Stone, Burial Bricks, Carved Burial Stone, slabs/stairs/walls.
Vanilla:
stone bricks, cracked stone bricks, cobblestone accents.

# R03 — MAIN BURIAL CHAMBER / ГЛАВНАЯ ПОГРЕБАЛЬНАЯ КАМЕРА

## Purpose
Prestigious burial room; one of the visual anchors of a Kurgan.

## Size
Small: 7×9 or 9×9.
Warrior: 9×11 or 11×11.
Great: 11×15, 13×15 or 15×17.

## Layout
The room MUST have a focal axis from entrance to tomb.

### Central dais
Raise the primary burial area by 1 block.
Use 2–3 slab/stair steps.

Dais size:
- small: 3×5;
- warrior: 5×7;
- great: 5×9 or 7×9.

### Sarcophagus / burial container
Centered or slightly back from exact center.
Never push it against random wall.

Surround by:
- 2 or 4 low offering points;
- urn niches;
- small candles/lamps.

## Floor design
Outer floor:
Cold Burial Stone / vanilla stone mix.

Inner ceremonial border:
Burial Stone Bricks or tile.

Dais:
Carved and clean Burial Stone variants.

Cracked blocks only outside the immediate main tomb unless using a ruined variant.

## Walls
Create bays every 3–4 blocks:
support pier
→ recessed niche
→ support pier.

Niches can contain:
- urn;
- decorative block;
- banner;
- empty shadow recess.

## Ceiling
Use visible structure:
- cross ribs;
- shallow vault impression;
- support strips from columns.

Great chamber should not have a flat single-material ceiling.

## Lighting
Warm low-level torch/fire/lantern placement around dais.
Do not make every wall bright.
Corners remain darker.

## Loot
Primary burial reward node.
Possible:
- sarcophagus loot;
- 2–4 side urns;
- one hidden niche in advanced variant.

Loot quality higher than ordinary side rooms.

## Enemies
If current Kurgan guardians exist, this is a valid guarded room.
Do not rebalance mob stats here.

## Connections
Usually 1 main entrance.
Warrior/Great may have 1 concealed or rear route, but main visual focus must remain burial.

## Used blocks
Custom:
Cold Burial Stone,
Burial Bricks,
Carved Burial Stone,
Cracked variant sparingly,
slabs/stairs/walls.

Vanilla:
stone bricks,
cracked stone bricks,
cobblestone only in damaged corners.

# R04 — WARRIOR BURIAL HALL / ЗАЛ ДРУЖИННИКОВ

## Purpose
Collective martial burial / retinue tomb.

## Size
9×11 to 13×17.

## Layout
Long hall with repeated side burial alcoves.

Main path:
3 blocks wide.

Both walls:
pairs of recessed alcoves every 3–4 blocks.

Alcove:
1–2 blocks deep;
2–3 wide;
2–3 high.

## Visual identity
More repetitive and military than the prestigious Main Burial Chamber.

Use:
- shield-like carved motifs;
- weapon-storage decoration only if current assets support it;
- low stone benches/platforms;
- burial urns;
- guardian spawn positions.

## Floor
Central runner of Burial Stone Bricks.
Side zones darker Cold Burial Stone.
Cracks increase toward far end.

## Ceiling
Regular support beams/ribs.
No ornate giant vault.

## Loot
Equipment-oriented:
existing Kurgan weapon fragments/materials;
coins;
repair/restoration materials;
occasional weapon/relic roll.

## Connections
Often through-route: 2 connectors.
May have one side secret.

## Used blocks
Custom:
Burial Stone Bricks dominant;
Cold Burial Stone;
Carved accents;
Cracked Burial Bricks.

Vanilla:
stone brick,
cobblestone,
cracked stone brick.

# R05 — TREASURE ROOM / СОКРОВИЩНИЦА

## Purpose
Clear reward space.

## Size
7×7 to 11×13.
Should be smaller and denser than a major hall.

## Entrance
Often protected by:
- narrower arch;
- trap room before it;
- secret side passage;
- guarded branch.

## Walls
Storage niches:
2-block-high recesses;
occasional shelf-like stair/slab arrangement.

## Floor
More intact than ruined corridor.
Use strong framing.

## Containers
Target:
- Small: 1 major + 0–2 minor containers.
- Warrior: 1–2 major + 2–4 minor.
- Great: 2–3 major + several side urn/caches, depending room variant.

Never line 10 chests against one wall.

## Props
Use:
- urns;
- burial boxes;
- relic stands;
- scattered storage;
- broken crate-like blocks only if they fit current project.

## Loot
High density but controlled.
Room should reward finding it.

## Trap variant
A chest or floor route may be trapped.
Visual clues must exist:
cracks, pressure plate seam, suspicious symmetry.

## Used blocks
Custom:
Burial Bricks,
Carved Burial Stone,
Cold Burial Stone,
slabs/stairs.

Vanilla:
stone bricks,
cracked stone bricks,
cobblestone in damaged storage corners.

# R06 — RITUAL HALL / РИТУАЛЬНЫЙ ЗАЛ

## Purpose
Ancient ceremonial chamber, not simply another tomb.

## Size
9×9 to 15×15.

## Shape
Prefer:
square;
octagonal impression created with blocked corners;
or rectangular with central ritual zone.

## Floor
Central 5×5 or 7×7 motif.
Use Carved Burial Stone and clean Cold Burial Stone.

Do not draw a giant noisy rune across the whole room.
Motif must be architectural and readable.

## Altar
At far wall or center depending variant.
2–4 blocks wide.
1–2 blocks high.

Use stairs/slabs to make layered altar profile.

## Walls
Symmetrical wall bays.
Carved blocks at eye level or above.
Optional damp/moss variant for abandoned ritual hall.

## Light
Warm fire on perimeter.
Optional cool/blue mystical accent only if existing project assets support it.
Do not flood entire room in cyan.

## Loot
Ritual ingredients;
existing relics;
ceremonial item rolls;
offerings.

## Used blocks
Custom:
Carved Burial Stone primary accent;
Cold Burial Stone;
Burial Bricks;
Mossy/Damp variant.

Vanilla:
stone bricks;
mossy stone bricks;
cracked stone bricks.

# R07 — TRAP GALLERY / ГАЛЕРЕЯ ЛОВУШЕК

## Purpose
A traversal hazard room.

## Size
5×11 to 9×17.

## Layout
Long enough to read danger before reaching reward/exit.

## Trap families
Use only mechanics currently implementable and fair:
- pressure trigger;
- projectile;
- falling block;
- collapsing floor/pit;
- false safe route;
- bait container.

Do not create unavoidable instant death.

## Visual clues
At least one clue:
- different floor tile;
- small holes in wall;
- cracked ceiling;
- suspicious plate;
- repeated missing grout.

## Floor palette
Burial Bricks plus selected tiles.
Trap trigger texture must not be completely invisible.

## Loot
Optional risk/reward:
small cache;
bait chest;
shortcut.

## Used blocks
Custom:
Burial Bricks,
Cracked Burial Bricks,
Cold Burial Stone slabs,
trap tile if already/currently justified.

Vanilla:
stone brick,
cobblestone,
gravel for collapse.

# R08 — FLOODED CRYPT / ЗАТОПЛЕННАЯ КРИПТА

## Purpose
Environmental variety.

## Size
7×9 to 13×17.

## Water
Shallow water preferred:
1 block deep across 20–60% of floor.
Occasional 2-block depression only in intentional section.

Do not fill the entire room to ceiling.

## Floor
Broken.
Some dry stepping zones.
Slabs and low platforms.

## Walls
Moss/damp variants at lower 2–3 blocks.
Drier blocks higher up.

## Source of flooding
Visually explain with:
- cracked side wall;
- ceiling seep;
- broken floor channel;
- natural rock intrusion.

## Loot
Dry ledge cache;
partially hidden urn;
secret behind water edge.

## Used blocks
Custom:
Mossy/Damp Burial Bricks,
Cracked Burial Bricks,
Cold Burial Stone.

Vanilla:
mossy stone brick,
stone,
cobblestone,
water.

bone-related materials;
hidden urn.

# R11 — SECRET RELIQUARY / ТАЙНЫЙ РЕЛИКВАРИЙ

## Purpose
Rare high-value secret.

## Size
3×5, 5×5, 5×7 or 7×7.
Never huge.

## Access
Behind:
- suspicious cracked wall;
- false niche;
- offset carved block;
- narrow secret connector.

## Visual identity
More intact and focused.
Strong central pedestal or single chest/cache.

## Loot
Higher quality per container than normal rooms.

## Blocks
Cleaner Carved Burial Stone.
Cold Burial Stone.
Burial Brick trim.
Minimal vanilla support.

# R13 — DESCENT HALL / ЗАЛ ПЕРЕХОДА НА НИЖНИЙ УРОВЕНЬ

## Purpose
One of several possible vertical connectors.

## Important
This room MUST NOT be the only repeated transition in every Kurgan.

## Variants
A. broad ceremonial stair;
B. side stair wrapping 90 degrees;
C. broken stair with short drop;
D. narrow shaft/ladder style if current mechanics allow;
E. hidden descent.

## Block usage
Stairs and slabs from Burial Brick and vanilla stone-brick families.
Carved blocks mark major Great Kurgan descent only.

## Lighting
Visible enough to understand route.
Not overlit.

---

# R14 — FINAL DEEP CHAMBER / ГЛУБИННАЯ КАМЕРА

## Purpose
Deep objective room where existing Kurgan boss/major encounter or final burial can occur.

## Scope
Do not redesign boss combat stats in 0.9.6.
Only make the room support the existing encounter.

## Size
Warrior: 11×13 to 15×17.
Great: 15×17 to 21×21 depending encounter needs.

## Requirements
- enough movement space;
- pillars/cover must not trap boss AI;
- no low ceiling clipping;
- clear entry;
- no chest directly beside entry;
- reward after encounter placed intentionally.

## Palette
Prestigious but darker than Main Burial Chamber.
Use Carved + Cold Burial + Burial Brick.
Vanilla blocks used as aged support.

# ROOM DISTRIBUTION RULES

Small Kurgan:
Required:
- vestibule;
- 1 burial-oriented chamber;
- 1–2 secondary rooms.
Optional:
trap, small treasure, ossuary, offering store, secret.

Warrior:
Required:
- vestibule;
- warrior hall OR guard-oriented burial room;
- main burial/deep chamber;
- 2–4 secondary rooms.
Strongly preferred:
treasure, trap, secret, crossroads.

Great:
Required:
- vestibule;
- crossroads/hub;
- main burial chamber;
- ritual OR warrior hall;
- treasure;
- at least one environmental room;
- final/deep chamber;
- multiple secondary rooms;
- secrets.

Do not make every Great Kurgan identical.
