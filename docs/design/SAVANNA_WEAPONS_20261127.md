# Savanna weapons — draft
20 new weapons plus existing upgraded Veles staff. Five direct trophy weapons, seven new crafted unique weapons, eight standard weapons. Polkan and Skarapeya drop one of their two weapons (50/50), Div claws guaranteed; materials on eligible kills. No ready animal weapons. Needle spends durability per projectile, no ammunition, 8 ticks ordinary shot, triple shot costs three, seven selectable poisons. Shared weapon input and paired rendering. First-pass balance. Pending models, textures, compilation, GameTests, client visual and animation QA. No class redesign.
