# 1.7.6: silhouettes defined before equipment integration

The supplied approved 64px sprites remain byte-identical inventory assets.

- Saber: long curved dark blade, thin gold cutting edge, hooked gold guard and red pommel tassel. Diagonal blade occupies roughly three quarters of the icon; narrow handle one quarter. Uses handheld vanilla slash poses, never a recolored straight sword.
- Shield: elongated red wooden heraldic face, stepped silver border and gold sun/crest. Its width is about half its height; raised rim and central emblem distinguish it from the vanilla rectangle. Blocking follows the vanilla raised shield pose.
- Idol chest: heavy protruding ivory shoulder plates, horizontal bone ribs and brown leather backing. Shoulders extend less than 3 model pixels.
- Marinka cloak: burgundy lined asymmetric split cloth tails, small collar; independently animated tail panels follow the body.
- Kartaus mantle: squared metal shoulders, high stiff collar, heavier straight back panels and central vertical red insignia.
- Belt: brown leather band, gold clasp and red embroidered tassels. Finist pendant: blue/gold bird emblem. Fang necklace: curved dark cord and four ivory fangs. Existing torso accessory attachment is reused.

Native player render checks have not yet been performed. Offline geometry checks do not substitute for client screenshots.
