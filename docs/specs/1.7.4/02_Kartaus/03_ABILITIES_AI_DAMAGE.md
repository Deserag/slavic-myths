# Kartaus — combat values, minions, defenses, phases, counterplay

## Baseline
Boss III tier, **650 HP**, attack rate moderate, melee staff range 3.2, walk speed 0.21 b/t, preferred player distance 7–13 blocks, 1.25 armor attribute scaling equivalent to mod standard (do not just copy Marinka attributes). Damage to 24-HP/12-heart diamond-armored player measured as **effective post-armor HP**. Serious direct hits must meet **>=6 effective HP (3 hearts)** absent active accessories/buffs; chip/minions intentionally lower. Avoid permanently bypassing armor: only one special spell may use partly magical damage as specifically specified. Boss bar red-black, RU name "Картаус Рыжий Ус"; clear HUD feedback for number of banners and cursed falcon.

## Phase and protection schedule
P1 100–65% HP: 0–1 banner, 1–2 guards, staff wave and chains; base damage reduction 10%. P2 65–30%: up to 2 banners, 2–3 guards; black eye + falcon curse; base reduction 10%. P3 <=30% (<=195 HP): 2 banners max, faster skills by 15% with MINIMUM windups unchanged, +25% spell damage (AFTER calculating effective damage carefully), limited guard replacements. No passive permanent invulnerability. Each ACTIVE banner adds 15 percentage points effective incoming damage reduction, cap **40% total**; remove ALL bonuses on banner destruction, but cannot take below zero base armor mitigation. At least once per phase player must have normal vulnerable interval 60t or longer; bar indicates 'Black banners: N/2'.

## AI targeting/decision constraints
STATE `IDLE -> INTRO (40t) -> CHOOSE -> CAST_<X> -> RECOVER -> CHOOSE`, `STAGGER (50-80t)` on banner/all banners broken, `RAGE_INTRO`, `DEAD`. Intro won't auto-grief. Priority per 20t AI planning scan: if no banner and cooldown ready => banner once; if fewer than 2 guards and enough distance => guard; if player cluster lined up => staff wave; if owner within 12=> black eye; if companion active and curse ready => curse; if melee threat <=3.3=> melee. RNG use stable seeded chooser to prevent repeating same skill >2 times consecutively, but never artificially delay attack if all cd. Cannot cast banners back-to-back at same point; >=5 blocks between banners; <=2 at once. Avoid minion targeting of different players except optional PvE multi participant logic; owner target remains first. Finist assistance triggers stagger when last banner removed during channel or when curse anchor freed (50t vulnerability). On last banner destroyed, no re-cast for **160t**; player gets 8 seconds to punish.

## Enemy economy and protection
Player should prioritize banners and guards. Kartaus cannot outrun melee player via perpetual teleports: there is NO teleport spell. Preferred distance can be maintained by slow sidesteps (<0.22 speed), line-of-sight rules. No minion drops or XP farming beyond vanilla baseline; suppress unique loot for generated guards if existing API provides. Entity cannot summon in lava/inside a house; fallback positions visible. Guards don't persist across event, don't convert villages via gameplay, don't count as normal biome mobcap.

## Quantified fairness checks
Benchmark a 24-HP player in FULL diamond with NO extra accessories: unblocked Staff Wave >=6 HP, staff melee >=6 HP, Black Eye >=6 HP; caution telegraph durations ensure dodge possible. Chains on own have lower damage but debilitating slow. Max simultaneous combo must not insta-kill 24-HP fully armored player with two overlapping unavoidable attacks. A script should ensure not more than one boss lethal AOE resolves within 24t of another. VFX rings give spatial data even when particles disabled.
