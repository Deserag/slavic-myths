## Explicit non-work
No cinematic moving camera, no fortress model, no siege camp template or loot chest, no altering villain names/lore for an unrelated source, no unrelated class rebalance. The design resembles an original Minecraft interpretation rather than copied commercial film voice/video assets.

## Acceptance priority
P0: entity summon, unique ownership, damage/AI, boss defeat, stable despawn/persistence, not-consumed call item, correct story restrictions, compile and in-world model visible.
P1: every listed combat ability and telegraph, companion/dive, active status and animations, sync/render, item sprite, boss spawn egg, audio.
P2: polish transitions, ambience, subtitles, rare VO scripts, CPU/performance edge cases; do not discard core to expand worldgen.
