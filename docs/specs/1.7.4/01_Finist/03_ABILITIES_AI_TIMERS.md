# Finist — mechanics, AI, cooldowns, priorities and co-op

## Fixed role and base stats
Max HP 170, movement base ~0.28 blocks/tick, combat range sword 2.7 blocks, follow desired owner 3–7 blocks. Damage normal sword 5 raw HP; this is intentionally BELOW dedicated damage heroes. Armor approx 12 (validate in mod’s attribute convention); knockback resistance 0.12. Attack wind-up 12t, recovery 14t. Active skills are designed to amplify PLAYER decisions; don't make Finist solo Kartaus. Default scout target selection is server within 20 blocks and owner has line of sight or companion visible path, query at most every 20t. Priority: owner immediately threatened > cursed falcon anchor > banner target > selected hostile > idle support.

## Falcon companion lifecycle
Single owned companion, never multiplied by reconnect, portal transition, chunk reload or morph. A lightweight child entity is optional; prefer synced bird renderer bound to hero if combat targeting does not require separate hitbox. It may be targetable during Kartaus curse but must not die from ordinary chip attacks. No collectible pet or taming mechanic added. Spawn/perch/remove as hero changes state. During curse, remove abilities Watch/Guard/Dive and mark Clear Sight as unavailable until anchor broken; Finist sword / inspiration remain available.

## Target and motion state machine
FOLLOW (owner <=24 blocks, path find every >=10t) -> GUARD/SCOUT -> ASSIST -> ABILITY_TELEGRAPH -> COMMIT -> RECOVER -> FOLLOW. If stuck for >80t and owner not in encounter, teleport near owner on solid walkable block within 4 blocks; not while taking boss damage or in a lock. In Kartaus encounter only, avoid banner center obstacles, keep <=24 blocks from owner. Do not repeatedly attempt skills with blocked LOS; back-off retry 20t. Hero never steals player's major kill credit; record participation in shared encounter state.

## Defense of owner
Watch provides advance warning but does not dodge FOR the player; Falcon Guard consumes long cooldown, and Inspiration does not heal HP. Player still responds to Kartaus floor targets and minions, may attack banner/anchor while Finist controls priority. Hero dying during scripted story encounter must fail safely and not softlock; last-stand withdraw preserves defeat condition.
