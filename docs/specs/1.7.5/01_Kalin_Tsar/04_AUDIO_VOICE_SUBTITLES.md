# Kalin-tsar — audio / voice / subtitles

## Voice direction
- Deep, harsh warlord voice
- Proud, imperious, confident early; furious late
- Short combat lines, no comedy

## Example subtitle events
- Summon elite: "Forward, my chosen guard!"
- Summon common troops: "Raise all troops! Crush them!"
- Mounted phase start: "Let the earth shake beneath my horse!"
- Rage phase: "No retreat! Drown them in steel!"
- Surrender: "You... have broken my host..."

## Required event sounds
- heavy armor steps
- sabre swings (light / heavy / spin)
- parry clang
- command shout
- war horn cue
- rage aura burst
- surrender collapse / knee impact
