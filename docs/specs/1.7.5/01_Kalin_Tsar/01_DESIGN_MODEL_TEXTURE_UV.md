# Kalin-tsar — design / model / texture / UV

## Core silhouette
Kalin-tsar must look like a dominant field commander, not a monster.
- Height: 2.8 blocks
- Broad shoulders, heavy torso, thick armored arms
- Long dark red cape with gold military symbol
- Heavy black/dark steel armor with gold trim and fur on shoulders
- Helmet with a red crest/plume
- Curved royal sabre as primary weapon

## Minecraft readability
- Strong blocky silhouette readable at medium distance
- Dark armor body, red cape, gold trim, red plume
- Face mostly hidden behind helmet; glowing red eyes acceptable if subtle
- Do not make him identical to Kartaus. Kalin is martial, not mystical.

## Materials / palette
Main: near-black steel, dark gray, crimson, muted gold, fur gray, leather brown
Accent: battle damage scratches, dull metallic edges, blood-darkened sabre details

## Texture guidance
- 32x32 minimum for simple pieces, 64x64 preferred for body/helmet/cape
- Avoid overly smooth gradients
- Use highlighted trim on chest emblem, sabre guard, cape border

## Equipment
- Royal sabre: wide curved blade, heavy guard, blood-dark groove acceptable
- Secondary side gear: belt ornaments, chain segment, commander insignia

## UV / geometry notes
- Keep cape readable from back; symbol centered vertically
- Shoulder fur should not clip with sabre swings
- Helmet crest must remain visible from side and back
