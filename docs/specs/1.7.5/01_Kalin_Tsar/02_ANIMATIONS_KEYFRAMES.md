# Kalin-tsar — animation keyframes

## Idle
- Heavy breathing
- Slight cape motion
- Sabre low-ready pose
- Head tracks target slowly

## Walk / run
- Slow confident walk when commanding
- Strong forward lean in charge
- Cape trails one or two frames after torso rotation

## Attack A: Overhead crushing strike
- Anticipation: raise sabre high over right shoulder (8–10 ticks)
- Commit: fast downward chop (4–5 ticks)
- Recovery: brief heavy pause (8 ticks)

## Attack B: Steel whirlwind
- Spread stance
- Full body rotation with 2–3 circular hits
- Advance slightly while spinning

## Attack C: Breakthrough charge
- Short crouch
- Burst dash forward
- Shoulder/sabre impact pose

## Defensive parry
- Quick weapon lift inward
- Sparks / block reaction
- Immediate short counter-cut

## Command animation
- Raise sabre upward and point toward battlefield edge
- Vocal order / war horn cue
- Used before elite/common waves

## Rage phase
- Faster head motion
- Sharper steps
- Stronger cape motion
- Reduced recovery frames

## Surrender ending
- Stagger
- Drop to one knee
- Sabre falls or lowers to the ground
- Head bows but entity remains alive / interactable for scene end
