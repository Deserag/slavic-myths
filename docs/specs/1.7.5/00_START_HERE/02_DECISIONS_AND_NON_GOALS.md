# Locked Decisions

- Kalin-tsar HP: **1800**
- Kalin-tsar height: **2.8 blocks**
- Mounted Kalin height together with horse: about **4.2 blocks**
- War horse height: **3.2 blocks**
- Final phase threshold: **below 20% HP (below 360 HP)**
- Kalin summons:
  - elite wave: 2–3 elite soldiers
  - common wave: 6–10 common soldiers
  - under 20% HP: mixed wave of elites + commons
- The boss is a **warlord**, not a magician.
- He may parry, charge, command, and summon troops, but not use occult clone/witch mechanics.
- He can begin mounted, then dismount during battle.
- Ending is surrender/capture, not a standard death collapse.

## Non-goals
- No unique rewards now
- No need to build a persistent giant capital or castle
