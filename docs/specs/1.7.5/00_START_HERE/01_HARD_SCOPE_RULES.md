# Hard Scope Rules

## In Scope
- One final boss: **Kalin-tsar**
- One companion entity/sub-entity: **Kalin's war horse**
- One compact battlefield/camp encounter
- One repeatable summoning flow for Kalin
- Support for choosing **exactly two** already-unlocked bogatyrs before battle
- Summoned waves of elite and common soldiers controlled by Kalin
- Health phase logic, mounted/dismounted flow, surrender ending
- Save-data flags for victory and battle participation

## Out of Scope
- No 1.7.6 rewards
- No full faction system
- No large procedural fortress generation
- No rewrite of existing hero systems
- No extensive cinematic questline
- No loot overhaul
- No redesign of older assets except summon item compatibility if strictly needed

## Performance / speed constraints
- Keep encounter implementation compact and modular
- Prefer reusing existing AI tasks and animation systems
- Limit max active summoned soldiers on the battlefield
