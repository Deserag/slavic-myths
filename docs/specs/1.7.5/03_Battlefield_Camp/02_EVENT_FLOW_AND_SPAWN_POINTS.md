# Battlefield event flow and spawn points

## Encounter start
- Player activates Kalin encounter item or trigger
- Camp appears or battle starts at prepared compact site
- Player chooses exactly two available bogatyrs before combat lock-in

## Spawn points
- Minimum 4 common-soldier edge spawn points
- Minimum 2 elite-soldier spawn points
- Keep pathing unobstructed toward the center

## Battle sequence
1. Intro / mounted Kalin enters
2. First mounted combat and elite pressure
3. Dismount transition
4. Increased common troop waves
5. Under 20% HP mixed desperation waves
6. Surrender end
