# Battle selection and save data

## Two bogatyrs rule
- Before battle, allow selecting exactly two unlocked bogatyrs from:
  - Ilya Muromets
  - Dobrynya Nikitich
  - Alyosha Popovich
  - Finist Yasny Sokol
- Lock the choice when battle starts
- No switching during battle

## Save-data
Store at minimum:
- kalin_battle_started
- kalin_battle_won
- selected_hero_a
- selected_hero_b
- times_kalin_defeated
- last_battle_timestamp (optional)

## Progress compatibility
This data must later support 1.7.6 reward distribution.
