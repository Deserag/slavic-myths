# War horse — design / model / texture

## Identity
Kalin's horse is a royal battle mount, heavily armored, menacing, but still believable as a horse.

## Scale
- Height: about 3.2 blocks
- Mounted total visual height: about 4.2 blocks

## Visual language
- Dark barding / armored horse cloth in crimson and black
- Gold trim and emblem matching Kalin
- Armored face mask
- Heavy saddle and decorated harness
- Strong front silhouette; horse must feel like a battering ram

## Texture notes
- 32x32 minimum, 64x64 preferred
- Red eyes acceptable but keep grounded; do not turn horse into a demon creature
- Distinguish metal armor, cloth barding, leather straps
