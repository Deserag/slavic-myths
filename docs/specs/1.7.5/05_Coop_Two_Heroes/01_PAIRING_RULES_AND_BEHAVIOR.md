# Two-hero pairing rules and behavior

Each hero keeps previously defined identity.

## Behavioral intent
- Ilya: tanks, intercepts, stabilizes dangerous melee pressure
- Dobrynya: parries, disciplined spear control
- Alyosha: attracts attention, dodges, annoys, opens punish windows
- Finist: support, warnings, falcon-assisted awareness

## In Kalin fight
- Heroes may temporarily switch from Kalin to spawned soldiers if player is surrounded
- They should not wander too far from the encounter center
- If one hero retreats at low HP, remaining hero continues; no replacement mid-fight
