# Balance windows and ending

## Windows of opportunity
Examples:
- After a failed mounted charge into obstacle / stop point
- After Steel Whirlwind recovery
- After heroes clear a wave and Kalin is isolated
- During surrender end state there is no further damage phase

## Ending
At 0 HP, Kalin does not die normally.
He kneels / surrenders.
Battle ends cleanly, soldiers stop hostility or despawn in controlled way.
Record victory.
