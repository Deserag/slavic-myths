# Content preservation during 0.9.4

No intentional content removals during 0.9.4 migration.

The port is incomplete. All baseline systems remain required, including the
recent Baba Yaga egg and placement fixes. PENDING inventory rows must not be
presented as registered/working target content. No source sets are excluded to
produce an artificial successful mod build; no gameplay stubs replace content.

Old Forge build files and metadata were replaced with their NeoForge equivalents.
The immutable pre-port checkpoint retains their originals for recovery/history,
not as a second maintained loader or platform.
